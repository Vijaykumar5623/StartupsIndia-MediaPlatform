Part 2

Screenshots to Capture

Take screenshots on a phone (Pixel or Samsung preferred). Minimum size: 1080×1920px. At least 2 required, 5 recommended. Capture these screens in order:

  • Home feed with news articles loaded and visible
  • Article detail screen — a full article open and scrolled slightly
  • Hero / Trending banner at the top of the home screen
  • Explore or Search screen
  • Profile screen showing saved articles or user details
Tip: Use a real device with real content loaded — not a simulator. Screenshots with actual articles look far more credible to Google reviewers.

Part 5

Step-by-Step Upload Instructions

Before you start: You need access to a Google Play Console account. If one hasn't been created yet, register at play.google.com/console — there is a one-time $25 registration fee.
1
Create the app
  • Play Console → All apps → Create app
  • App name: StartupsIndia
  • Default language: English (India) — en-IN
  • App or game: App
  • Free or paid: Free
  • Accept the declarations → Create app
2
Fill the store listing
  • Left menu → Store presence → Main store listing
  • Paste app name, short description, full description from Part 1
  • Upload the app icon (512×512 PNG)
  • Upload the feature graphic (1024×512 PNG)
  • Upload at least 2 phone screenshots from Part 2
  • App category: News & Magazines
  • Fill in contact email and website URL → Save
3
Add the privacy policy
  • Left menu → Policy → App content → Privacy Policy
  • Enter URL: startupsindia.in/privacy
  • Save
4
Complete the Data Safety form
  • Left menu → Policy → Data safety
  • Work through each section using the answers from Part 3
  • Save and submit
5
Complete content rating
  • Left menu → Policy → App content → Content rating
  • Start questionnaire → Category: News
  • Answer using Part 4 above → Submit
  • Rating is generated automatically — no further action needed
6
Set target audience
  • Left menu → Policy → App content → Target audience
  • Select: 18 and above
  • Save
7
Upload the AAB
  • Left menu → Release → Production → Create new release
  • Under "App integrity" — when Google asks about Play App Signing, click "Use Google Play's app signing key" and accept. This is mandatory and completely normal.
  • Upload the .aab file provided by the development team
  • Release name: 1.0.0
  • Release notes: Initial release of StartupsIndia
  • Save → Review release
8
Send for review
  • Fix any items marked as Errors (Warnings can be skipped for now)
  • Click "Start rollout to Production" → Rollout
  • Google review typically takes 1–3 business days for a new app
  • You will receive an email when approved or if any changes are needed
If Google rejects the app — they will give a specific reason in Play Console and via email. Forward the rejection message to the development team and it will be resolved promptly.
